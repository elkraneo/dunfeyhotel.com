# Contributing

Bug reports and fixes welcome.

Two standing policies, not open for PRs:

1. **No Apple content in the repo.** Snapshots, transcripts, artwork,
   normalized datasets — none of it gets committed here, ever. The build
   fetches from Apple's services; `data/` stays gitignored. PRs that commit
   data will be closed.
2. **Link, don't host.** The site links every video, transcript, document and
   download to its source. Features that rehost Apple content won't be merged.
